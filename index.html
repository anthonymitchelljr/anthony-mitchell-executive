Executive Profile

Business-minded technology leadership

Anthony L. Mitchell Jr. is a technology executive with more than two decades of progressive leadership across enterprise infrastructure, cybersecurity, cloud platforms, governance, compliance, and IT operations.

His career includes technology leadership at Bridger Photonics, the American Petroleum Institute, and Dynex/Magellan Biosciences. Across these environments, he has guided modernization programs, improved resilience, strengthened governance, and built trusted partnerships with executive and operational stakeholders.
