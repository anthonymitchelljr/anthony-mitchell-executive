Executive Perspective

My first five months as a CIO.

A CIO mandate requires more than a traditional 90-day plan. My approach balances trust, risk reduction, operational stabilization, strategic alignment, financial discipline, and measurable execution.

Month 01

Listen

Meet leaders, assess risk, understand talent, architecture, service performance, and enterprise priorities.

Month 02

Stabilize

Address immediate reliability, cybersecurity, compliance, continuity, and service-delivery gaps.

Month 03

Align

Translate strategy into a technology agenda and partner with the CFO on spend, contracts, cloud, capital, and forecasts.

Month 04

Mobilize

Publish the roadmap, sequence investments, and launch priority modernization, security, data, and operating-model initiatives.

Month 05

Measure

Track budget performance, savings, vendor commitments, cloud costs, return on investment, and transformation momentum.
